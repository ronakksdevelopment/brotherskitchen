Our Story

A family table, open to everyone

Brothers Kitchen & Family Restaurant is a casual, affordable, home-style multi-cuisine spot (Indian, Indo-Chinese, and all-day breakfast) built for solo diners, big groups, and families with kids alike.

Since opening in 2024, the kitchen has built its name on signature specialties: Matka Biryani, Matka Chicken, and Bamboo Biryani, served alongside classic thalis, gravies, and evening platters. Beyond everyday dining, Brothers Kitchen regularly hosts birthdays, anniversaries, kitty parties, and family get-togethers.
